Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what customers ask us most.

How fast can you install internet at my home?
For most areas in Jeetpur-Simara, Parsagadhi, and Sakhuwaparsauni, we can complete installation within 24–48 hours of your request. Just call or WhatsApp us at +977 9802596081.
Do you provide IPTV service?
Yes! We offer IPTV packages with hundreds of Nepali, Indian, and international channels in HD quality. Bundled with our internet plans at a discounted rate.
Do you provide CCTV cameras with mobile viewing?
Yes. All our CCTV installations come with mobile remote viewing so you can monitor your home or business from anywhere using your smartphone.
Where can I visit in person to buy your services?
You can visit our authorized Point of Sale at Ghimire General tatha Dudh Dairy Pasal, located at Jeetpur-Simara-7, Jeetpur Tedha Road, Bara. Our friendly staff there can help you sign up for internet or CCTV services.
What areas do you serve?
We serve the entire Jeetpur-Simara Sub-Metropolitan City, Parsagadhi Municipality, and Sakhuwaparsauni Rural Municipality. Check our Service Area tab to check your exact location on the map.
Do you offer maintenance for existing CCTV systems?
Absolutely. We service and repair CCTV systems even if they were installed by someone else. We also offer routine maintenance contracts for businesses.
